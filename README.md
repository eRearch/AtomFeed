## What Is This

AtomFeed is a daily literature intelligence service for **researchers**.

Every morning at 8:00, your inbox receives an email containing only the latest papers **truly relevant to your research direction**, with AI-generated bilingual summaries (CN/EN) and key experimental data extraction.

Not pushed by an algorithm. Defined by you.
